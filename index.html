FAQ

Something else on your mind? info@getreal.money

Is it free?

Yes, the core app is free. Here's how we make money.

Is my data safe?

Your bank connections go through Plaid, the same service many banking apps use. Get Real can read your accounts but can't move money. Your data is encrypted, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

Do you sell my data?

No. Never.

How do you choose partners?

We only work with licensed partners for insurance and lending, never more than two in a category, and we only show one when it beats what you already have.

Which banks work?

Most US banks, credit unions, and card issuers, through Plaid.

Who is it for?

People earning a real income who still feel behind: carrying debt, living close to paycheck to paycheck, or just without a plan they trust.
